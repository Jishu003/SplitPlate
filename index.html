The Problem

Group orders are a mess

Every time your squad orders food together, one of these three things happens:

💸
One person pays everything
Then spends days texting "bro send ₹312" — and gets back ₹0 before eventually giving up.
🧮
Painful manual splitting
Scrolling through itemised bills, mental arithmetic, someone always forgets the delivery fee.
😤
Everyone orders separately
Multiple orders, multiple delivery fees, multiple delivery times. The food arrives cold and in stages.
The Solution

SplitPlate does the math for you

Tag items to people. The bill splits automatically. Everyone pays exactly what they owe.

1

Everyone picks their items

Browse Multiple restaurants, tag each item to the right person — all in one cart.

2

Bill splits automatically

Each person's exact amount: their items + proportional fees + 5% tax. Order nothing → pay ₹0.

3

Collect via QR codes

Send each person their QR payment card. Track who paid, who hasn't. No awkward texts.

4

Simultaneous delivery

Ordered from 2 restaurants? 2 delivery partners dispatched at the same time.
